Payment Link & Checkout Generator

PayPal • Debit / Credit Card guest checkout • Apple Pay • Google Pay

PayPal uses PayPal Payments Standard; a fixed currency_code must match the currency supported by the receiving PayPal account.
Required: Turn on PayPal Account Optional in PayPal → Account Settings → Website payments → Website preferences. Card processing, Apple Pay and Google Pay run inside PayPal’s hosted secure checkout. This page intentionally never asks for raw card numbers.
Clicking the generated Debit / Credit Card button opens PayPal’s secure checkout. The buyer sees the card details form when guest checkout is available for that transaction. No card data touches your website.
